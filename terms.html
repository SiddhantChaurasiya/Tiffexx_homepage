About Tiffexx

Tiffexx is a technology platform that connects customers with independent tiffin partners (kitchens) and delivery partners for the ordering and delivery of meals and meal subscriptions. Tiffexx facilitates these transactions; the tiffin partner is responsible for preparing the food.

2

Eligibility & your account

You must be at least 18 years old to use Tiffexx. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for all activity under your account. Provide accurate information and keep your delivery addresses up to date.

3

Orders & subscriptions

Orders are subject to the tiffin partner being open and accepting orders within the applicable meal time window.
Meal subscriptions generate daily delivery orders according to the schedule and meal preferences you select. You may pause, resume, or cancel a subscription from the app, subject to the applicable cut-off times.
Prices shown include applicable taxes (GST). A tax invoice is issued for each delivered order.
A tiffin partner or Tiffexx may cancel an order in exceptional circumstances (for example, if an item is unavailable); in such cases any amount paid is refunded to your wallet.
4

Payments & wallet

Payments on Tiffexx are made using your Tiffexx wallet. You can add money to your wallet using Razorpay. Wallet balances are non-transferable and may be used only to pay for orders and subscriptions on Tiffexx. Refunds for cancelled or undelivered orders are credited back to your wallet.

Wallet balances are non-transferable and dedicated exclusively to Tiffexx orders and subscriptions.
5

Cancellations & refunds

You may cancel an order before the tiffin partner accepts it for a full refund to your wallet. Once preparation has begun, cancellation may not be possible. If an order is not delivered or is materially deficient, contact support and we will review the issue and refund where appropriate.
