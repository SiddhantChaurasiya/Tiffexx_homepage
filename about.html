What Tiffexx is

Tiffexx is a technology platform that connects customers with independent tiffin partners — home kitchens and small food businesses — and with delivery partners who bring the food to your door.

The kitchen cooks the food and is responsible for it. Tiffexx handles everything around that: discovery, ordering, payment, delivery and support.

Step by step

How it works

Four simple steps from local kitchen to your dining table.

01

Browse kitchens

Browse the kitchens that deliver to your address. Each one delivers within 6.5 km of where it cooks.

02

Order & schedule

Order a single meal, or start a meal plan that arrives on a schedule you choose.

03

Tiffexx Wallet

Pay from your Tiffexx Wallet. The bill shows the food, taxes and any delivery fee before you confirm.

04

Follow on map

Follow your delivery partner on the map until the tiffin reaches you.
